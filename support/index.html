支持

「节日祝福」帮你记住要祝福的人和日子，节前提醒，编辑贺卡并通过系统分享发出。无需登录，业务数据只存本机。

常见问题

  • 没有收到提醒：请到「我的 → 提醒设置」确认已开启，并在系统设置中允许通知。
  • 贺卡无法保存到相册：请允许「添加照片」权限，或改用系统分享发送。
  • 开屏广告：仅在你同意隐私政策后展示非个性化广告；可在「我的 → 隐私与协议」撤回同意。
  • 会员购买 / 恢复：使用同一 Apple ID 点击「恢复购买」；订阅管理请到 App Store → 账户 → 订阅。
  • 清除数据：「我的 → 清除全部数据」可删除本机全部业务数据。

Support

Fest Bless helps you remember people and festivals, set local reminders, edit cards, and share via the system share sheet. No account required; business data stays on device.

Quick help

  • No reminders — enable in Profile → Reminder settings and allow system notifications.
  • Can’t save to Photos — allow “Add Photos”, or share instead.
  • Ads — non-personalized app-open ads after privacy consent; withdraw under Profile → Privacy.
  • Purchases — Restore with the same Apple ID; manage subscriptions in App Store → Account → Subscriptions.

Refunds

Request via reportaproblem.apple.com or Apple Support.

Contact

Use the developer contact on the App Store product page. Include iOS version, App version, and steps to reproduce.
